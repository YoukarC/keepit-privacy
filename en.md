# KeepIt Privacy Policy: where are my things

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**
**Applies to the Android app (Google Play), package `com.youkar.keepit`.**
**Publisher: Youkar.** Contact: YoukarC@hotmail.com

> This text describes accurately what the app does. It is not legal advice.

## In one sentence

KeepIt works with no account and no server: everything you note in it stays on
your phone. We collect no data.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter or create: your places and containers, items, quantities,
notes and tags, photos, projects, loans, label designs and settings. It is all
stored in a local database in the app's private storage, which other apps
cannot access. Uninstalling KeepIt erases it permanently.

## No collection, no tracking

- No account, no email address, no password.
- No analytics, no usage measurement, no automatic crash reports.
- No ads and no advertising ID.
- No tracking, no third-party component watching how you use the app.

The app itself makes no network call. Nothing is sold, rented or passed on to
anyone: there is no mechanism to do so.

**Local usage counter.** The "My usage" screen shows your active days, scans
and searches. These figures stay on the device, are never sent, and you can
export them as CSV if you wish.

## The KeepIt+ purchase and Google Play

KeepIt+ is a one-time, lifetime purchase. Payment is handled entirely by
Google Play under Google's own policy: we receive neither your name nor your
payment details. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase was
made.

The Google Play Billing library adds the network permissions (`INTERNET`,
`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) and the billing permission (`BILLING`) to the app.
KeepIt uses them for nothing else.

## Permissions, and why

- **Camera**: read the QR codes on your labels in Put away mode and photograph
  your places and items. Images are analysed on the device (Google ML Kit
  barcode scanning, provided through Google Play services): no image leaves
  your phone. Optional.
- **Notifications**: the loan reminders you set, triggered by your phone,
  never by a server. Optional.
- **Device start-up**: reschedule those reminders after a restart.
- **Vibration**: haptic feedback for scans and notifications.
- **Photos**: to pick an existing image, KeepIt uses the system picker; the
  app does not get access to your whole photo library.

KeepIt does not ask for access to your location, contacts or microphone.

## Backups and household sync

You can export your data to a ZIP archive and keep it wherever you like. It
can be **encrypted (AES-GCM) with a passphrase** kept in Android's secure
storage (Keystore); if you lose it, nobody, including us, can open the file.

The optional household sync uses a folder that **you** choose (for example a
folder synced by your own cloud service). KeepIt writes and reads a file
there; the transfer between devices is done by that folder's service, not by
KeepIt. Once the file leaves the app, that service's policy applies.

## Sharing

Label sheets (PDF) and shopping lists are shared only when you ask, through
Android's share sheet: you choose the app and the recipient. Nothing is sent
automatically.

If the Domio app is installed, KeepIt can open it through a direct link; no
data is passed on.

## Children

KeepIt is aimed at a general audience, not at children.

## Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability.
Here you exercise them directly in the app:

- **Access and rectification**: everything is shown and editable in the app.
- **Portability**: the backup export produces a file you can take with you.
- **Erasure**: delete an item or place in the app, or uninstall KeepIt to
  erase everything.

We hold no copy of your data, so we could neither provide it nor delete it on
your behalf.

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
